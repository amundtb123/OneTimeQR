OneTimeQR – QR codes that expire

Share files, text and links behind a QR code that disappears after a set time or number of scans. Set an expiry, share the QR, done.

Features

  • Share once: the content can be opened one time, or as many times as you choose.
  • Expiry from 10 minutes up to 7 days, then the content is deleted automatically.
  • Combine files, text and URLs in one QR code.
  • Password, view-only mode, a separate QR key, or two QR keys that must both be scanned.
  • Gift with a date: the content opens on a date you choose.
  • Encrypted in your browser. We never see the key. No account needed to get started.

Pricing

Text, links and all settings are free. Files are free up to 5 MB without an account and up to 10 MB when logged in. Larger files cost 1.5 kr per GB of traffic.
